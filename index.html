UNDERSTAND THE CONCEPT

Encoding ≠ Encryption ≠ Hashing

ENCODING

Changes data into another representation. Usually reversible without a secret key.

ENCRYPTION

Protects confidentiality using an encryption algorithm and key.

HASHING

Produces a fixed-length digest commonly used for integrity checks and password systems.
